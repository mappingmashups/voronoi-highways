Interstate Palette Lab

Every point in the lower 48 is coloured by its nearest north–south (odd) and nearest east–west (even) Interstate. Pick a palette, build your own, or let the map drift through all of them.

The Interstate Electoral College
The 54 zones of the major-routes map as states, replaying the 2024 presidential election.
Hover or tap a zone. 270 to win.

Population: Census 2020 block groups. Votes: 2024 county results, shared out within each county by population. Each zone gets 2 electoral votes plus House seats by Huntington–Hill, for 538 in all (Alaska and Hawaii aren’t on the map). Winner takes all; deeper colour means a wider margin.

Regions are nearest-route Voronoi cells built from Natural Earth 1:10m roads (with label fixes), measured in Conus Albers. Route numbers map onto a fixed 0–100 scale on both axes, so colours stay comparable between the two networks. Saved palettes stay in this browser only.
